# Help center — Personal Discipline

Common questions about the app. Each answer is short and matches what the app does today. Question not answered here? Send a message to the admin from **Me → Contact the admin**.

## Getting started

### How do I sign up?
Sign up with an email and a password (at least 8 characters). When you sign up you agree to the terms of use and confirm you are 16 or older. Sign-up is free.

### I have an invite code. What does it give me?
With an invite code from a friend, you both get 50 coins. The friend who invited you gets another 50 coins when you complete a first week in the table. The code is optional.

### How do I install it on my phone?
On Android (Chrome), open the browser menu ⋮ and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**. On iPhone (Safari), tap the Share button (a square with an arrow pointing up), scroll down and choose **Add to Home Screen**. Once installed, the app opens full screen and works offline.

### What is email verification and why do I need it?
To post in the community, comment or start challenges, you need to verify your email. Enter the 6-digit code we sent to your inbox from the **Me** screen. This keeps the community free of fake accounts.

### How do I turn on two-step verification?
You can turn on two-step verification by email in your account and security settings. After you turn it on, every sign-in sends a code to your email in addition to your password.

## Today and the weekly table

### How do I mark a task?
On the **Today** screen, tap a task to mark it. Each tap cycles through V (done), X (missed), - (not relevant) and empty. The tasks are breakfast, lunch, dinner, sport and a weekly weigh-in on Sunday.

### How is the V% calculated?
V% counts only cells marked V or X. Cells with text are not counted, and vacation and rest don't count as workouts.

### How do I move between weeks or fix an earlier week?
Move between weeks with the arrows or by swiping. You can go back and fix earlier weeks, and you can also open a missing week by its date.

### How do I write text in a cell, such as "Vacation"?
Double-tap the cell and type free text. A cell with text is not counted in V%.

### How do I lock a day or a week?
Tap the day or week in the table and choose lock. A locked day can't change by accident, not even through the assistant. You can unlock it at any time.

### What is a freeze?
In the store you can buy a day freeze or a week freeze. A freeze keeps your streak when a day or week goes by without marks. You can freeze only a day from the last 14 days, and only a week from the last 5 weeks.

### How do I earn coins?
A full day earns 5 coins, a workout 10, the daily challenge 15 and a week's streak 20. Invite coins are paid for up to 5 friends.

### What can I buy with coins?
Coins buy skins, colors, assistant characters, profile frames, and items such as streak freezes and extra assistant messages. Coins are part of the game only. They have no cash value and can't be exchanged for money.

### How do levels work?
Your level is based on all the coins you have ever earned. Store purchases don't lower your level, and each level unlocks perks.

### How does the assistant work?
The floating assistant button is off by default, and you can turn it on in Settings. The assistant answers questions about your data and can mark the table if you ask ("mark lunch V for me"). It does not give medical advice, and its answers can be wrong.

### Does the assistant see my password?
No. Your question and a summary of your data are sent to OpenAI to generate an answer. Passwords and contact details are not sent.

### How do ready-made training plans work?
Choose a plan, such as "First run: 5 km in 8 weeks" or "Home strength, 3 times a week". Pick your training days and today's session appears on the **Today** screen. Marking the session fills the sport row. If you miss a day, the next session waits for the next training day.

## Workouts and GPS

### How do I record a workout with GPS?
Choose **Start a GPS workout**. Your phone measures distance, time, pace and the route on a map. Keep the app open, because the browser doesn't track location when the screen is off.

### Why are the step counts an estimate?
Steps are calculated from distance, so they are an estimate only.

### Can I share a workout without showing my location?
Yes. A workout story shows only the shape of the route, smaller, with no map and no coordinates. The full route stays in your account and is shown only to you.

### What happens to my location when I get a "good time to run" tip?
An approximate location from your last workout is sent to the Open-Meteo weather service, with no identifying details.

### How do I import workouts from my watch?
Connect your Strava account, and workouts from your watch (Garmin, Apple Watch, Polar, Coros, Suunto and more) come in with distance, time and route. The connection must be set up by the app admin. You can disconnect at any time, and the key is deleted.

## Stories and community

### How long does a story last?
A story disappears after 24 hours. You can add up to 5 gallery photos at once, or a workout card, an achievement or text.

### How do people react to my story?
Friends react with a high five instead of a like. A private reply to a story is visible only to the story's owner, and the owner can see who viewed the story.

### What is the difference between a post and a story?
A post stays in the feed and can have up to 4 photos, text, likes and comments. For each post you choose whether it is for the whole community or friends only. You can delete a post at any time, and deleting it also deletes its photos.

### What happens if I write a post without a connection?
The post or story is saved on your device and sent automatically when you're back online, even if you closed the app in the meantime. Up to 20 posts can wait.

### What is a meetup?
A meetup is a group run or workout. You post it in the community and people mark **I'm going**. You can add it to your calendar and get a reminder on the day.

### How do I report content?
Open the **⋯** menu on a post or story and choose report. The report goes to the admin, who can see the reported content. Content with three reports is hidden until the admin reviews it.

### What is not allowed?
Offensive, harassing, racist or sexual content. Photos of other people without their consent, other people's personal details, ads and spam, and encouraging extreme fasting, purging or other dangerous behavior.

### How do I contact the admin?
Go to **Me → Contact the admin** to ask a question or report a problem. The reply comes to the app and to your email. You can send up to 10 messages a day.

## Challenges and leagues

### What kinds of challenges are there?
"Who wins" challenges, where people compete on workouts, runs, healthy meals, full days or coins, and **team goals**, where everyone works together toward one target. Scores are calculated automatically from each participant's table. The winner of a challenge with friends gets 100 coins.

### How do I join a challenge?
Join with a challenge code. You can run up to 5 active challenges at the same time. Each challenge has its own chat, and you can leave a challenge at any time.

### What is a weekly league?
About 15 people at a similar level compete every week. The top 3 move up a league and the bottom 3 move down. Leagues are in **Community → Challenges**.

### What are personal challenges?
Daily, weekly, monthly and yearly challenges, with badges for completing them. Each completed challenge earns coins.

## Music

### How do I connect Spotify?
Connect your account once, and you can see your playlists, search for songs and control playback. Controlling playback from inside the app requires Spotify Premium. Without Premium you get an embedded player for the playlist.

### Where does the music play?
Playback comes from the Spotify app that is open on your phone, computer or speaker. The access key is stored only on your device, and you can disconnect at any time on the Music screen.

### Can I use music without connecting Spotify?
Yes. Paste a link to a song, album or playlist from Spotify or Apple Music, and the player appears on the workout screen too. If the Spotify connection isn't turned on in the app yet, the link still works.

## Privacy and blocking

### Where is my data stored?
Your data is stored in a private spreadsheet and folder in the Google Drive account of the app operator. A copy is kept on your device so the app works offline. The app doesn't use advertising cookies or third-party tracking tools.

### Who can see my data?
The admin sees only summary statistics: workouts, meals, weight change and activity. The admin doesn't see your notes, weekly photos or personal details. The leaderboard shows your name, photo, level and coins.

### Who can see my photos and stories?
For each post you choose whether it is for the whole community or friends only. Photos are stored in a private folder and shown only to people allowed to see the post. Stories disappear after 24 hours, and the owner can see who viewed them.

### How do I block someone?
Open the **⋯** menu on a post or story and choose block. After blocking, you won't see each other's posts, stories, comments or messages, and the blocked user isn't notified.

### How do I unblock someone?
Blocked users are listed in Settings, where you can unblock them.

### How do I delete my account?
In Settings, choose delete account. This permanently deletes all your data: weeks, photos, coins and messages. It can't be restored, so export a backup first if you want to keep anything.

### How do I see or correct my data?
You can export all your data at any time from Settings. For a request to see, correct or delete your data, contact the admin through **Contact the admin**.

## Backup and import

### How do I export a backup?
In Settings you can export JSON, which includes GPS workouts, extra trackers and goals, and you can export CSV to open in a spreadsheet. It's a good idea to export a backup now and then.

### How do I restore a backup?
JSON import restores a backup you exported earlier. Importing a Word file brings in an old table: weeks, weight and marks load with a preview before anything is saved.

### Is my data backed up automatically?
The app operator backs up the data every night. The app is provided as is, free and in beta, so export your own backup too.

## Settings, accessibility and language

### How do I change the language or the way the app addresses me?
The app is available in Hebrew or English. The app and the assistant address you in the masculine or feminine form, as chosen in your profile, or you can choose not to say.

### How do I turn on dark mode?
Use the quick button in the header to switch between light and dark mode. You can also choose an app color in Settings.

### What accessibility options are there?
You can increase text size, turn on high contrast, highlight links, use a readable font, add more text spacing and stop animations. The app supports keyboard navigation and screen readers. The accessibility statement includes a form for accessibility questions.

### How do I get reminders?
You can get a reminder to weigh in on Sunday and a daily reminder. If your browser doesn't support notifications, the reminder appears inside the app.
